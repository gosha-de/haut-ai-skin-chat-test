# Test assignment: Senior Product Designer

**Skin.Chat returning user**

Thank you for your interest in the Senior Product Designer role at Haut.AI! This take-home assignment is the next step after your interview with us.

**The short version:** a shopper talked to Skin.Chat on a brand's website a few weeks ago and got a skincare routine. Design why and how they come back, what they get when they do, and how they see their progress since the last visit.

**Time:** about one working day. **Tools:** Claude Design or Claude Code, not Figma.

## Context

Skin.Chat is our AI skincare assistant. Beauty brands add it to their website much like any support chat (Intercom, Ada, Zendesk, etc.): a chat widget that opens from a button on any page. It can also live in messengers like WhatsApp and Instagram, but for now the main focus is the brands' e-commerce websites.

Skin.Chat answers skincare questions, analyses skin from a selfie (skin scores like wrinkles, redness, pores, etc.) and recommends a routine from the brand's own catalog, with add to cart right in the chat. It remembers the conversation and the last analysis, so the shopper can continue where they left off.

## The problem

Many shoppers do one consultation and never come back. But skincare is not a one-time thing: a routine takes weeks to show results, products run out, and skin changes with the season. So there are real reasons to come back. A "We miss you!" reminder is not one of them.

## Try it yourself

[skin.chat](https://skin.chat) is the public version of Skin.Chat. It isn't a brand version, but it's the same assistant. Please open it on your phone, take the skin analysis, get a routine and look at what the chat remembers. No login needed.

## The task

The shopper did a skin analysis on a brand's website 3 weeks ago, got a routine of 3 products and bought 2 of them in the chat. Please answer four questions with your design:

1. **How do they come back?** Which touchpoints, in which channel, and when. Pick one path and design it end to end; a short list is enough for the others.
2. **Why do they come back?** The reason has to be value for the shopper, not a reminder.
3. **What do they get when they come back?** What the chat says first, what it already knows, and what the useful next step is.
4. **How do they see progress since the last visit?** For example, a new selfie compared to the last one. Please think about what happens when nothing changed or the scores got worse.

Show it in one brand style of your choice, and say what a brand could change and what stays fixed.

**Not in this assignment:** the first visit, the selfie capture flow itself and the brand's admin.

## Assumptions

- The chat remembers the last conversation, the last skin results and scores, the recommended routine and what was bought in the chat.
- The brand can reach the shopper only in channels where the shopper agreed to it (email, WhatsApp, etc.).
- Skin changes slowly.
- Skincare only.
- Mobile first. It must work for many brands without custom design work for each one.

You're welcome to add your own assumptions; just list them in the rationale.

## Tools

All work is done in Claude Design, Claude Code or both. Please don't use Figma for this assignment, including for the first sketches. If you don't have a Claude plan, let us know before you start.

## What to send

1. **A clickable prototype** of your main return path that opens on a phone, from the touchpoint to the progress view and the next step. A shared Claude Design link, or a hosted link to what you built in Claude Code (plus the repo). The chat answers and the scores can be scripted; no real AI needed.
2. **One page of rationale:**
   - The reasons to come back you considered, and why you picked yours
   - Touchpoints: which ones and when, and when to stop sending
   - What you looked at in other products (if anything)
   - How we would know it works: one metric (for example, the share of shoppers who come back within 30 days) and one cheap test with real people
3. **Half a page on how you worked with Claude:** what you asked it to do, what you changed by hand and where it didn't help. Please attach the main prompts or a session export.

We don't expect every edge case. Depth on the main moments matters more than the number of screens.
